# Play Store listing: English

**App name** (30 max): Bible Connections

**Short description** (80 max):
See how the whole Bible connects: 61,000+ cross references on one map.

**Full description** (4000 max):

Bible Connections draws every cross reference in the Bible as an arc, from Genesis to Revelation, so you can see at a glance how the story fits together.

Tap any verse to see where it points, then follow the links. Search for a verse, a chapter, a book or a word, and the map takes you there.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Explore more than 61,000 connections between passages, filtered by how strong they are or which books they join
• Take guided journeys, such as The Lamb of God, Holy Week and Advent, with leader notes for groups
• Follow reading plans, with progress saved on your phone
• See Bible places and routes on a map, including Paul's journeys
• Trace prophecies and where the New Testament quotes the Old
• Study the Hebrew and Greek words behind a verse
• Save verses, write notes and build collections, then turn them into slides or a handout
• Read and listen to any passage in your favorite translation on Bible.com

MADE FOR CHURCHES AND GROUPS
Share a sermon series page with your church, print a handout for small groups, or show the map on the big screen.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no adverts and no cookies. Your notes and saved verses stay on your device. The app is free.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
English, Spanish and Portuguese, with the right Bible translation for each.

ABOUT THE TEXT
To respect copyright, Bible Connections does not store any Bible translation. Reading links open Bible.com. Cross reference data comes from OpenBible.info and original-language data from STEP Bible (both CC BY 4.0).
