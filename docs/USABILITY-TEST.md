# Usability test script

A short test with five people finds most of the problems. Each session takes about 20 minutes. Use a phone for at least three of the five.

## Who to ask

- Two people who read the Bible most weeks but do not study it in depth.
- Two small group leaders or youth leaders.
- One person who rarely reads the Bible.

## Before you start

- Open https://bibleconnections.app in a private window, so nothing is saved from earlier.
- Say: "We are testing the site, not you. Please think out loud: say what you expect, what you look at and what surprises you. There are no wrong answers."
- Do not help. If someone is stuck for more than a minute, note it and move on.

## Tasks

Read each task aloud. Note where people hesitate, what they tap first and anything they say.

1. **First look (no task).** "Take 30 seconds to look at this page. What do you think it does?"
2. **Find a verse.** "Find out which passages connect to Psalm 23."
3. **Open a connection.** "Pick one of those connections and read both passages."
4. **Share.** "Send that connection to a friend." (They can stop at the share sheet.)
5. **Go back.** "Go back to everything connected to Psalm 23."
6. **Guided journey.** "Find a short guided tour you could use with a small group, and start it."
7. **Settings.** "Change the Bible translation to one you prefer."
8. **Your own place.** "Save Psalm 23 so you can find it again next week. Now find it again."

## After the tasks

Ask:

- "What was the most confusing moment?"
- "What would you use this for, if anything?"
- "Is there anything you expected to find but did not?"

## What to record

For each task: finished or not, time taken, and the first thing they tapped. A task that two or more people fail or hesitate on is a problem to fix first.

## Measures to compare between rounds

- Tasks 2 and 3 finished without help.
- Time to finish task 2.
- The first-look answer matches what the site does.
