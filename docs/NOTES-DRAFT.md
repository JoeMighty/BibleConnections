# New connection notes: draft for review

These notes are drafts. They are not on the site yet. They cover the strongest connections that have no note, starting with the most agreed-on links.

For each one, mark **Approve**, **Change** (with your wording) or **Remove**. Approved notes move into `site/content/notes.json` and appear on the site, then are translated.

Each note follows the content principles in [THEOLOGY-REVIEW.md](THEOLOGY-REVIEW.md): it explains why the passages connect, in plain words, without quoting any translation, and labels the kind of link honestly.

## 1. Phil.1.6 and Eph.4.12 (Same truth)

Paul is sure God will finish the good work He began in His people. In Ephesians he shows one way God does it: He gives leaders to prepare His people for service so the church grows up.
