About Bible Connections

Every arc joins two passages of Scripture that point to each other. Short arcs connect nearby verses, long arcs stretch across the whole story of the Bible, from Genesis to Revelation.

How to use it

  • Scroll, pinch or use the + and − buttons to zoom. Drag to move around.
  • Tap an arc to see the two passages, then read them on Bible.com.
  • Tap the bars along the bottom to see every connection in that chapter.
  • Search for a reference or a word, pick a theme, or take a guided journey.
  • Use Share to send someone the exact connection you are looking at.
