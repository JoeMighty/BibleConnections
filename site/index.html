          About Bible Connections

          Version · What’s new · · How to use · Roadmap

          Created by JoeMighty · Source code on GitHub

          Every arc joins two passages of Scripture that point to each other. Short arcs connect nearby verses, long arcs stretch across the whole story of the Bible, from Genesis to Revelation.

          How to use it

          • Scroll, pinch or use the + and − buttons to zoom. Drag to move around.
          • Tap an arc to see the two passages, the words they share and why they connect, then read them on Bible.com.
          • Open any verse to study it: its original Hebrew or Greek words, its context, connections, places and questions. Add your own notes, print a handout or share it as an image.
          • Tap the bars along the bottom to see a chapter, with its key words and places.
          • Search for a reference or a word, pick a theme, take a guided journey, or explore word studies and places in Study.
          • Use the headphones button to listen on Bible.com, where the translation has audio.
          • In Settings, choose your translations, arc colors, text size, an easier-to-read font, animations, and back up or save everything for offline.
